     1→     1→# TA Workbench — Forex & Binary Options Technical Analysis Platform
     2→     2→
     3→     3→A user-defined technical-analysis platform: **you** create, organize, version and
     4→     4→evaluate your own trading strategies. The platform is *not* a signal generator,
     5→     5→has no built-in mandatory indicators or trading rules, executes **no orders**,
     6→     6→and **never fabricates market data**.
     7→     7→
     8→     8→**Phase 5** added the **Live Analysis Dashboard** — a
     9→     9→professional, responsive surface for viewing real market data and explainable
    10→    10→analysis:
    11→    11→
    12→    12→* an interactive **candlestick chart** (TradingView Lightweight Charts v5,
    13→    13→  Apache-2.0): crosshair OHLC readout, zoom/scroll, visible price & time
    14→    14→  scales, theme-aware colours, responsive resizing, and honest "forming —
    15→    15→  not final" labelling of the in-progress candle;
    16→    16→* a **polling candle feed** (`useCandleFeed`) on the documented candle API
    17→    17→  (no WebSocket exists in the backend): configurable intervals with
    18→    18→  visibility-aware pausing, duplicate/out-of-order protection
    19→    19→  (closed candles replace their forming predecessor), at most one request
    20→    20→  in flight, and full cleanup on unmount;
    21→    21→* honest **connection & freshness indicators**: Live / Stale / Not connected /
    22→    22→  No candles / Failed — "live" is claimed only while the last fetch
    23→    23→  succeeded and the backend's own data-quality gate says the data is fresh;
    24→    24→  after a failure the last REAL candles stay visible but the chart stops
    25→    25→  claiming to be live;
    26→    26→* **chart overlays derived exclusively from the analysis engine's output**
    27→    27→  (`src/lib/chart/overlay-model.ts`): swing highs/lows with confirmation
    28→    28→  timing, HH/LH/HL/LL labels, BOS segments, MSS/CHoCH levels and FVG zones
    29→    29→  from condition evidence, user-defined strategy price zones — each overlay
    30→    30→  traceable to the report that produced it. Concepts the engine does not
    31→    31→  detect (order blocks, breaker blocks) are listed as engine-unsupported and
    32→    32→  are never drawn; overlays are per-timeframe and the frontend never
    33→    33→  re-computes market geometry;
    34→    34→* the **strategy evaluation panel**: strategy + immutable version selection,
    35→    35→  four-state condition sections (met / not met / inconclusive / unsupported),
    36→    36→  regime + timeframe agreement, invalidation & confirmation rules, data
    37→    37→  warnings and the full persisted report — no probabilities, no confidence
    38→    38→  percentages, no profitability claims;
    39→    39→* mode separation end-to-end: **Forex** defaults to D1/H4/H1/M15/M5/M1;
    40→    40→  **Binary Options** defaults to M1-only (higher timeframes appear only when
    41→    41→  the evaluated strategy explicitly requires them) and states plainly that
    42→    42→  MT5 data is not the broker's execution feed and OTC prices are never
    43→    43→  simulated.
    44→    44→
    45→    45→**Phase 3** built the **Custom Strategy Builder**: user-managed categories,
    46→    46→subcategories and strategies with ownership scoping (`X-User-Id` header,
    47→    47→default `local`), a **draft → validate → publish** workflow with immutable,
    48→    48→versioned rule sets, a versioned rule-set schema 2.0
    49→    49→([`docs/strategy-rule-schema.md`](docs/strategy-rule-schema.md)), a rule-type
    50→    50→registry and a professional Strategy Builder UI.
    51→    51→
    52→    52→**Phase 4** adds the **Explainable Technical Analysis
    53→    53→Engine** on top of that foundation:
    54→    54→
    55→    55→* a deterministic, documented **market feature library** (`features.py`):
    56→    56→  candle anatomy, SMA/EMA/ATR, confirmed swing points with EXPLICIT
    57→    57→  confirmation timing, HH/HL/LH/LL structure, historical structure breaks,
    58→    58→  zone interactions and data completeness — every feature publishes its
    59→    59→  definition, required history and limitations (`GET /api/v1/analysis/features`);
    60→    60→* a configurable **market regime classifier** (`regime.py`): uptrend,
    61→    61→  downtrend, range, breakout, transition and uncertain — explicit,
    62→    62→  testable heuristics with full evidence, never probabilities;
    63→    63→* a **four-state rule engine**: every condition reports
    64→    64→  `met` / `not_met` / `inconclusive` / `unsupported` — AND never converts
    65→    65→  unknown or unsupported conditions into success, OR never conceals
    66→    66→  unavailable conditions; closed candles by default with an explicit,
    67→    67→  labelled opt-in for forming-candle (provisional) analysis;
    68→    68→* four **advanced rule types promoted to operational** with precise
    69→    69→  documented definitions and tests: `mss_choch`, `bos`, `liquidity_sweep`,
    70→    70→  `fvg` (order blocks, breaker blocks and S/R reactions remain PLANNED and
    71→    71→  rejected by validation);
    72→    72→* the **engine run + persisted reports**: `POST /api/v1/analysis/run` fetches
    73→    73→  real candles for exactly the strategy's required timeframes, classifies the
    74→    74→  regime (with higher/lower-timeframe agreement — never forced), evaluates
    75→    75→  every condition and stores a versioned, auditable report (schema 1.0)
    76→    76→  with per-timeframe provenance, staleness and warnings. Binary Options
    77→    77→  runs state clearly that MT5 is not the broker's execution feed and that
    78→    78→  OTC data is never simulated;
    79→    79→* an **Engine run (MT5)** tab in the frontend: strategy + symbol, run,
    80→    80→  distinct accessible visual states for all four condition outcomes, regime
    81→    81→  and data panels, limitations, and full analysis history.
    82→    82→
    83→    83→Automatic order execution and profitability claims remain **out of scope
    84→    84→forever**. The report describes technical conditions on historical data —
    85→    85→it is not a prediction and carries no probability of profit.

    89→    89→## Product principles
    90→    90→
    91→    91→| Principle | Implementation |
    92→    92→|---|---|
    93→    93→| User-defined strategies | Users build their own categories, strategies, conditions, AND/OR groups, confirmation & invalidation rules. Nothing is imposed. |
    94→    94→| Explainable analysis | Every evaluation returns a per-condition tree: met / missing, numeric evidence, criteria and data-quality warnings. |
    95→    95→| Explicit whitelist | Condition types, operators, fields, group logic, modes and timeframes are enumerated server-side (`/api/v1/meta`); anything else is rejected with precise error paths. |
    96→    96→| Versioned rule sets | Saving a rule set creates a NEW immutable **draft** version (`strategy_versions.status`); publishing freezes a definition and supersedes the previous one. History is never rewritten — ready for later backtesting. |
    97→    97→| Ownership | Every category/strategy is scoped to an owner (`X-User-Id` header; default `local`). Foreign-owned records are indistinguishable from missing ones (404 — no existence leak). |
    98→    98→| No arbitrary code execution | Rule sets are pure data interpreted by a deterministic evaluator. No `eval`, no user expressions. |
    99→    99→| Forex / Binary Options separation | Separate modes everywhere: API filtering, rule validation and default timeframe profiles (Forex: D1, H4, H1, M15, M5, M1). Binary Options strategies **default to M1**; higher timeframes are used only when the user explicitly declares them — never forced. |
   100→   100→| Honest market data | If MetaTrader 5 is not connected the API and UI say so plainly and serve NOTHING. Every candle payload carries `data_provenance` (`mt5_live` / `postgres_cache`), `data_status` and data-quality warnings (gaps, duplicates, invalid OHLC, staleness). Forming candles are flagged and can be excluded with `closed_only=true`. |
   101→   101→| No fabricated prices | The MT5 adapter is the only data source; missing history is reported, never back-filled, interpolated or synthesized. |

   131→   131→### MT5 integration topology (important)
   132→   132→
   133→   133→The `MetaTrader5` Python package only runs where the terminal runs.
   134→   134→
   135→   135→- **Recommended**: run the backend **natively on the Windows host** with the
   136→   136→  MT5 terminal installed, running and logged in. `pip install -r
   137→   137→  requirements-mt5.txt`, set `DATABASE_URL` (plus optional `MT5_*` settings),
   138→   138→  start uvicorn. The backend attaches to the terminal via its IPC bridge.
   139→   139→- **Wine**: possible with Windows Python under Wine, unsupported here — test
   140→   140→  carefully before relying on it.
   141→   141→- **Docker Compose (Linux containers)**: MT5 **cannot** run inside the
   142→   142→  container. Compose therefore starts the backend with `MT5_ENABLED=false`
   143→   143→  (safe disabled mode — API and UI report it honestly). PostgreSQL and the
   144→   144→  rest of the platform keep working; alternatively point the frontend at a
   145→   145→  backend running on a Windows machine via `NEXT_PUBLIC_BACKEND_ORIGIN`.
   146→   146→- **Security model**: credentials (if any) exist ONLY in environment
   147→   147→  variables; no endpoint accepts credentials, terminal paths or anything
   148→   148→  executable; the terminal is never exposed to the public internet; if the
   149→   149→  API is reachable remotely, put it behind your own auth proxy / VPN
   150→   150→  (built-in authentication is a later phase) and restrict the network.
   151→   151→- **Timestamps**: candle times are preserved exactly as MT5 reports them —
   152→   152→  epoch seconds whose wall-clock value is the BROKER SERVER time (the MT5
   153→   153→  convention). Every response also renders the same epoch as ISO-8601 UTC
   154→   154→  (`time_utc`) and carries `timezone: "MT5_SERVER"` with a human-readable
   155→   155→  `timezone_note`. Nothing is ever re-based silently.

   215→   215→## API conventions
   216→   216→
   217→   217→Every response uses the same envelope (`app/core/responses.py`):
   218→   218→
   219→   219→```json
   220→   220→// success
   221→   221→{ "success": true,  "data": { ... }, "error": null,
   222→   222→  "meta": { "request_id": "…", "timestamp": "…", "api_version": "v1" } }
   223→   223→
   224→   224→// error
   225→   225→{ "success": false, "data": null,
   226→   226→  "error": { "code": "RULE_VALIDATION_FAILED", "message": "…", "details": [ { "path": ".conditions[0].operator", "message": "…", "code": "unsupported_operator" } ] },
   227→   227→  "meta": { "request_id": "…" } }
   228→   228→```
   229→   229→
   230→   230→The `request_id` is also returned in the `X-Request-ID` header and appears in
   231→   231→the server's structured logs — quote it when reporting issues.

   233→   233→### Endpoints
   234→   234→
   235→   235→| Method & path | Purpose |
   236→   236→|---|---|
   237→   237→| `GET /health/live` | Liveness (no dependencies). |
   238→   238→| `GET /health/ready` | Readiness: PostgreSQL probe + market-data status (503 when DB down). |
   239→   239→| `GET /api/v1/meta` | Modes, default timeframes, rule-format whitelists, market-data info. |
   240→   240→| `GET /api/v1/market-data/status` | Honest MT5 availability report (live check). |
   241→   241→| `GET /api/v1/mt5/status` | MT5 terminal/account connection report + safe config summary. **No credentials ever.** |
   242→   242→| `GET /api/v1/market/symbols` | Symbols known to the terminal (`?search=EUR*&limit=100`). |
   243→   243→| `GET /api/v1/market/candles` | Validated OHLCV series (`?symbol=&timeframe=&limit=&closed_only=&refresh=`). |
   244→   244→| `GET /api/v1/market/tick` | Latest tick / bid-ask / spread for one symbol. |
   245→   245→| `GET /api/v1/market/diagnostics` | End-to-end diagnostics: connection + live candle probe + capabilities. |
   246→   246→| `GET/POST /api/v1/strategy-categories` | List (nested, `?mode=&include_archived=`) / create categories & subcategories (`parent_id`). |
   247→   247→| `GET/PATCH/DELETE /api/v1/strategy-categories/{id}` | Manage a category (delete refused while dependents exist). |
   248→   248→| `POST /api/v1/strategy-categories/{id}/archive` \| `/unarchive` | Non-destructive organisation (archive cascades to subcategories). |
   249→   249→| `GET /api/v1/strategies` | Paginated listing (`?mode=&category_id=&status=&include_archived=&page=&page_size=`) → `{items, page:{page,page_size,total,pages}}`. |
   250→   250→| `POST /api/v1/strategies` | Create a strategy (owner-scoped; archived categories refused). |
   251→   251→| `GET/PATCH/DELETE /api/v1/strategies/{id}` | Detail (current rule set + human-readable summary) / update / delete. |
   252→   252→| `POST /api/v1/strategies/{id}/duplicate` | Deep-copy metadata + latest rule set as a new draft (`{name?, include_rule_set?}`). |
   253→   253→| `POST /api/v1/strategies/{id}/archive` \| `/unarchive` | Archive / restore (publish is blocked while archived). |
   254→   254→| `POST /api/v1/strategies/{id}/validate` | Validate a rule set against the schema; the result is persisted (audit trail). |
   255→   255→| `POST /api/v1/strategies/{id}/versions` | Validate + save a NEW immutable **draft** version. |
   256→   256→| `GET /api/v1/strategies/{id}/versions[/{n}]` | Version history (with `status`: draft/published/superseded) / specific version. |
   257→   257→| `POST /api/v1/strategies/{id}/versions/{n}/publish` | Publish a draft version: freezes the definition, supersedes the previous published one, strategy → `active`. |
   258→   258→| `POST /api/v1/rule-sets/validate` | Validate a rule set without saving (editor support). |
   259→   259→| `GET /api/v1/rule-types[/{type}]` | The rule-type registry: parameter schemas, semantics, availability (`available:false` = planned, NOT operational). |
   260→   260→| `POST /api/v1/analysis/evaluate` | Explainable evaluation on user-supplied candles (accepts stored versions or inline rule sets; schema 1.0 and 2.0). |
   261→   261→| `POST /api/v1/analysis/run` | **Phase 4 engine run**: fetch real candles for the strategy's required timeframes, classify the regime, evaluate every condition, persist a versioned report (201). Refuses with 503 when MT5 cannot supply data — never fabricates. |
   262→   262→| `GET /api/v1/analysis/reports` | Analysis history for the owner (`?symbol=&strategy_id=&mode=&setup_status=&page=&page_size=`). |
   263→   263→| `GET /api/v1/analysis/reports/{id}` | Retrieve one persisted report (full payload; owner-scoped 404 for foreign owners). |
   264→   264→| `GET /api/v1/analysis/features` | Documented catalogs: market features, regimes, volatility states, strategy buckets. |
   265→   265→| `GET /api/v1/analysis/readiness` | Engine readiness: DB, rule-registry coherence, live provider status (`ready` vs `live_analysis_ready`). |
   266→   266→
   267→   267→**Ownership.** Every strategy/category endpoint is scoped to the requester.
   268→   268→The owner is taken from the optional `X-User-Id` header (1–64 chars,
   269→   269→`[A-Za-z0-9._@-]`; absent/empty → `local`). Requests for another owner's
   270→   270→records return 404, exactly like missing records — no existence oracle.
   271→   271→This is data scoping, not authentication (see Known limitations).
   272→   272→
   273→   273→Structured errors from the market endpoints:
   274→   274→
   275→   275→| HTTP | Code | When |
   276→   276→|---|---|---|
   277→   277→| 422 | `VALIDATION_ERROR` | Bad symbol/timeframe/limit (precise `details[].path`). |
   278→   278→| 404 | `SYMBOL_NOT_FOUND` | Symbol unknown to the terminal. |
   279→   279→| 502 | `MARKET_DATA_ERROR` | Terminal returned an unusable/empty response. |
   280→   280→| 503 | `MARKET_DATA_UNAVAILABLE` | MT5 disabled, package missing or terminal unreachable. |

   311→   311→
   312→   312→**Caching & data distinction.** Raw MT5 responses are validated in-process
   313→   313→(`validation.py`) and — when fresh enough per timeframe — served from the
   314→   314→PostgreSQL `candles` cache (`data_provenance: "postgres_cache"`); otherwise
   315→   315→they are fetched live (`mt5_live`) and persisted. Duplicate inserts are
   316→   316→impossible (composite PK + upsert); the full history is never loaded per
   317→   317→request; a dedicated `refresh=true` request bypasses the cache. No Redis.

   319→   319→Interactive docs: `http://localhost:8000/docs` (disabled when `APP_ENV=production`).

   321→   321→### The versioned rule format (schema `2.0`, legacy `1.0` supported)
   322→   322→
   323→   323→Full specification: [`docs/strategy-rule-schema.md`](docs/strategy-rule-schema.md)
   324→   324→(+ generated JSON Schema at `docs/strategy-rule-set.schema.json`).
   325→   325→
   326→   326→```jsonc
   327→   327→{
   328→   328→  "schema_version": "2.0",
   329→   329→  "mode": "forex",                          // forex | binary_options
   330→   330→  "timeframes": ["H1", "M15"],              // must be declared here first
   331→   331→  "direction": "bullish",                   // bullish | bearish | both
   332→   332→  "symbols": ["EURUSD"],                    // optional scope (absent = any)
   333→   333→  "market_regime": "trending",              // optional applicability
   334→   334→  "evaluation": { "on_close_only": true },  // optional settings
   335→   335→  "conditions": {                           // root group (required)
   336→   336→    "logic": "AND",                          // AND | OR
   337→   337→    "conditions": [
   338→   338→      { "id": "trend-hold", "type": "moving_average", "timeframe": "H1",
   339→   339→        "lookback": 60, "optional": false,
   340→   340→        "params": { "ma_type": "ema", "period": 20, "compare_to": "value",
   341→   341→                     "value": 1.08, "operator": "above" } }
   342→   342→    ],
   343→   343→    "groups": [ /* nested AND/OR groups (max depth 4) */ ]
   344→   344→  },
   345→   345→  "confirmation": { "logic": "AND", "conditions": [ /* … */ ] },  // optional
   346→   346→  "invalidation": { "logic": "AND", "conditions": [ /* … */ ] },  // optional
   347→   347→  "notes": "free-form user documentation (optional)"
   348→   348→}
   349→   349→```
   350→   350→
   351→   351→**Rule-type registry** (single source of truth: `rule_registry.py`, served at
   352→   352→`/api/v1/rule-types`). Operational in Phase 3: `price_level`,
   353→   353→`candle_property`, `candle_pattern`, `swing_point`, `structure_pattern`
   354→   354→(HH/HL/LH/LL), `market_structure_break`, `moving_average`, `price_zone`.
   355→   355→Planned — documented but **NOT operational**, rejected with
   356→   356→`rule_type_not_available`: `mss_choch`, `liquidity_sweep`, `fvg`,
   357→   357→`order_block`, `breaker_block`, `support_resistance_reaction`. Every
   358→   358→operational type has defined semantics, input validation and tests; a unit
   359→   359→test guarantees registry ↔ evaluator consistency.
   360→   360→
   361→   361→**Legacy 1.0** rule sets (flat `price_level` conditions, direction without
   362→   362→`both`) remain valid and evaluatable — stored strategies from earlier phases
   363→   363→keep working unchanged.
   364→   364→
   365→   365→**Evaluation semantics** (deterministic, documented, tested — see the schema
   366→   366→doc for per-type details):
   367→   367→
   368→   368→- Group `AND` = every **required** child satisfied (unmet *optional* rules
   369→   369→  are reported but never block); `OR` = at least one child satisfied.
   370→   370→- Result status: `conditions_met`, `conditions_missing`, `invalidated`
   371→   371→  (invalidation group satisfied), or `insufficient_data` (referenced
   372→   372→  timeframe has no usable candles — the platform never invents data).
   373→   373→- **No look-ahead bias**: evaluators only use information contained in the
   374→   374→  supplied candles. Swing confirmation requires `strength` subsequent
   375→   375→  candles *inside the data*; a candidate near the right edge never counts.

   658→   658→## Phase 6 — Historical analysis, backtesting & strategy performance
   659→   659→
   660→   660→The Backtests tab runs a saved, immutable strategy version over the stored
   661→   661→historical candle cache and reports setups and (only where explicitly
   662→   662→configured) simulated trades. Full design notes: `docs/backtest-schema.md`.
   663→   663→
   664→   664→Core guarantees (enforced by tests):
   665→   665→
   666→   666→- **Chronological, zero look-ahead** — the evaluation of candle *i* sees
   667→   667→  only candles whose CLOSE time ≤ the close time of candle *i* on every
   668→   668→  timeframe (higher-timeframe candles still forming at that instant are
   669→   669→  invisible). Swing/structure confirmation timing is inherited from the
   670→   670→  shared Phase 4 evaluator, so a break can never be backdated before it
   671→   671→  was knowable. The evaluator's wall-clock stamp is replaced with the
   672→   672→  simulated instant, making runs byte-for-byte reproducible.
   673→   673→- **Setup-only by default** — trades are simulated only when the request
   674→   674→  carries explicit execution assumptions (Forex: spread/slippage/
   675→   675→  commission/SL/TP/sizing/swap + same-candle SL/TP policy; Binary:
   676→   676→  expiry candle count, payout, stake, tie policy). `direction: both`
   677→   677→  strategies never get a fabricated trade side — setups are recorded,
   678→   678→  trades are not.
   679→   679→- **Declared assumptions only** — every fill derives from stored candle
   680→   680→  prices plus the persisted cost configuration; a candle touching both
   681→   681→  stop and target follows the configured policy (conservative default /
   682→   682→  optimistic / ambiguous-and-excluded). Nothing favourable is assumed
   683→   683→  silently.
   684→   684→- **Metrics come from records** — `win_rate` = wins / (wins + losses)
   685→   685→  (denominator documented in the payload); missing data renders as
   686→   686→  "Not available", never zero; `profit_factor` is undefined (null) without
   687→   687→  both gross sides; equity/drawdown derive from closed-trade balances only.
   688→   688→- **No profitability claims** — every metrics payload states these are
   689→   689→  historical simulation statistics, not expected future performance; MT5
   690→   690→  data is never described as a broker's execution feed and OTC feeds are
   691→   691→  never simulated.
   692→   692→
   693→   693→API (owner-scoped, standard envelope):
   694→   694→
   695→   695→| Method | Path | Purpose |
   696→   696→| --- | --- | --- |
   697→   697→| POST | `/api/v1/backtests` | validate + queue a run |
   698→   698→| GET | `/api/v1/backtests` | paginated run history (filters: mode, strategy, status, symbol) |
   699→   699→| GET | `/api/v1/backtests/{id}` | configuration, status, metrics |
   700→   700→| GET | `/api/v1/backtests/{id}/results` | paginated chronological records (filters: kind, setup_status, outcome) |
   701→   701→| GET | `/api/v1/backtests/{id}/results/{seq}` | one record with the full evaluation tree |
   702→   702→| GET | `/api/v1/backtests/{id}/equity` | realized equity path (empty for setup-only runs) |
   703→   703→| POST | `/api/v1/backtests/{id}/cancel` | cooperative cancellation (next evaluation boundary) |
   704→   704→| GET | `/api/v1/backtests/{id}/export` | CSV export (results or summary) with formula-injection protection |
   705→   705→| GET | `/api/v1/backtests/data-coverage` | pre-run candle availability check |
   706→   706→
   707→   707→Execution architecture: runs execute as in-process `asyncio` tasks
   708→   708→(`BACKTEST_MAX_CONCURRENT`, default 1) — a documented, minimal runner, no
   709→   709→external task queue. Runs left queued/running by a restart are marked
   710→   710→`failed` with code `RUNNER_RESTARTED` at startup (never silently "done").
   711→   711→
   712→   712→Relevant settings: `BACKTEST_MAX_CANDLES` (window cap, default 20 000),
   713→   713→`BACKTEST_WARMUP_CANDLES` (lookback warm-up before `start`, default 150),
   714→   714→`BACKTEST_MAX_EXPIRY_CANDLES` (binary expiry cap), `BACKTEST_MAX_CONCURRENT`.

   716→   716→## Phase 7 — AI-assisted strategy analysis & explainable research
   717→   717→
   718→   718→Phase 7 adds an **evidence-first AI research layer** that explains the
   719→   719→deterministic engines' recorded results in plain language — and nothing
   720→   720→more. Full design notes: `docs/ai-research.md`.
   721→   721→
   722→   722→Architecture (strictly separated):
   723→   723→
   724→   724→- **Deterministic engines stay authoritative** — the analysis engine and
   725→   725→  backtest engine are the only source of conditions, four-state statuses,
   726→   726→  evidence and metrics. The LLM never calculates values, never evaluates
   727→   727→  rules, never decides whether an unsupported rule "passed".
   728→   728→- **Minimized, validated context** — the model receives a pydantic-validated
   729→   729→  JSON context: mode/symbol/timeframes, data freshness and quality warnings,
   730→   730→  regime + evidence, the exact immutable strategy version, every condition
   731→   731→  with its recorded status and a stable evidence ref, backtest metrics
   732→   732→  verbatim (`null` = "not available", never zero-filled) and explicit
   733→   733→  `unavailable` items. No credentials, no raw candles, no other users' data.
   734→   734→- **Fail-closed validation** — every answer passes schema validation, an
   735→   735→  evidence-reference check (dangling refs → discarded), numeric
   736→   736→  traceability (invented numbers → discarded) and safety guards
   737→   737→  (guarantees / confidence percentages / order advice / code → rejected).
   738→   738→  Failed answers are never shown as success: `502 AI_OUTPUT_INVALID` plus
   739→   739→  an honest persisted `error` message for chats.
   740→   740→- **Prompt-injection protection** — user text is untrusted data confined in
   741→   741→  `<user_message>` delimiters, control-character-stripped, size-capped and
   742→   742→  secret-redacted before it reaches the provider or logs. The LLM has no
   743→   743→  tools, no code execution and no database access.
   744→   744→- **Provider abstraction** — any OpenAI-compatible endpoint works
   745→   745→  (`AI_BASE_URL` + `AI_MODEL`, key server-side only); bounded timeouts,
   746→   746→  retries and rate limits; quota/outage/malformed responses mapped to
   747→   747→  honest errors. `AI_ENABLED=false` (default) keeps the whole deterministic
   748→   748→  platform fully usable; the UI explains that AI is unavailable and why.
   749→   749→
   750→   750→API (owner-scoped, standard envelope):
   751→   751→
   752→   752→| Method | Path | Purpose |
   753→   753→| --- | --- | --- |
   754→   754→| GET | `/api/v1/ai/status` | honest AI-layer status (booleans only — no secrets) |
   755→   755→| POST | `/api/v1/ai/explanations` | explain one authorized analysis report |
   756→   756→| POST | `/api/v1/ai/research/chat` | one research-chat turn (persisted, auditable) |
   757→   757→| GET | `/api/v1/ai/research/conversations` | list the owner's conversations |
   758→   758→| POST | `/api/v1/ai/research/conversations` | create a conversation |
   759→   759→| GET | `/api/v1/ai/research/conversations/{id}` | retrieve a conversation + messages |
   760→   760→| DELETE | `/api/v1/ai/research/conversations/{id}` | delete an authorized conversation |
   761→   761→
   762→   762→Frontend: a violet-accented **AI explanation panel** under every engine
   763→   763→report (AI-generated interpretation visually distinct from deterministic
   764→   764→evidence, every cited item shows its evidence refs) and an **AI Research**
   765→   765→tab with owner-scoped conversations, visible research context and
   766→   766→loading/empty/error states.
   767→   767→
   768→   768→Passing validation proves grounding to the supplied evidence — **not**
   769→   769→factual market truth. No confidence percentages are produced (qualitative
   770→   770→uncertainty only), and no statement is ever a prediction or advice.

   774→   774→## Troubleshooting
   775→   775→
   776→   776→| Symptom | Cause & fix |
   777→   777→|---|---|
   778→   778→| Backend exits: `DATABASE_URL …` | Config invalid/missing. Copy `backend/.env.example` → `backend/.env` and set a real PostgreSQL URL. |
   779→   779→| Backend exits: `MT5_LOGIN … MT5_PASSWORD/MT5_SERVER` | Partial MT5 credentials. Provide all three or none (recommended: none — attach to the logged-in terminal). |
   780→   780→| `/health/ready` returns 503, database `down` | PostgreSQL unreachable. `docker compose up -d db`, or let `make backend` provision a local pgserver cluster. The app keeps running and reports honestly. |
   781→   781→| Frontend shows “backend unreachable” | Backend not started. Run `make backend` (or `docker compose up -d backend`). |
   782→   782→| `/api/v1/mt5/status`: package “not installed” | The `MetaTrader5` package is missing or you are on Linux/macOS. Install `backend/requirements-mt5.txt` on the Windows host with the terminal. The API stays honest — no data is faked. |
   783→   783→| `/api/v1/mt5/status`: “Could not connect … after N attempt(s)” | Terminal not running/not logged in. Start & log into MT5, wait ~30s (reconnect cooldown), then press “Check now”. |
   784→   784→| `MARKET_DATA_UNAVAILABLE` (503) with `MT5_ENABLED=false` | Disabled mode (e.g. Docker Compose default). Enable MT5 on a Windows host or run analysis on user-supplied candles. |
   785→   785→| `SYMBOL_NOT_FOUND` | The symbol is unknown to this terminal. Check `GET /api/v1/market/symbols`; some brokers suffix symbols (`EURUSD.i`). |
   786→   786→| `MARKET_DATA_ERROR` “no rate history” | The symbol exists but has no data for that timeframe/history depth. Try another timeframe or a smaller `limit`. |
   787→   787→| Candle response says `STALE_DATA` / `GAPS_DETECTED` | The terminal is serving old or gapped history (market closed, low liquidity, or symbol recently selected). Warnings are informational; nothing is back-filled. |
   788→   788→| Port 8000 already in use | Another backend instance is running — `make backend` detects and skips. Change `API_PORT` if needed. |
   789→   789→| `rule-sets/validate` returns `RULE_VALIDATION_FAILED` | Read `error.details[].path` — each entry pinpoints the exact violation against the whitelist. |
   790→   790→| Rule save rejected: `rule_type_not_available` | The rule set references a PLANNED type (e.g. `fvg`, `mss_choch`). These are documented but not operational. Pick an available type from `GET /api/v1/rule-types`. |
   791→   791→| `strategy_mismatch` on validate | The rule set's mode/direction disagrees with the strategy (a `both` strategy accepts any rule-set direction; a directional one does not). |
   792→   792→| Publish returns `ALREADY_PUBLISHED` / `VERSION_SUPERSEDED` | That version is already the live definition, or a historical one. Save a new draft version instead. |
   793→   793→| Publish returns `STRATEGY_ARCHIVED` | Unarchive the strategy first. |
   794→   794→| `INVALID_OWNER_ID` | The `X-User-Id` header must be 1–64 chars from `[A-Za-z0-9._@-]`. |
   795→   795→| A strategy “disappeared” from the list | It (or its category) is archived. Enable “Show archived”, or `?include_archived=true` / `?status=archived`. |

   797→   797→## Known limitations (Phase 4)
   798→   798→
   799→   799→1. **Ownership is scoping, not authentication** — the `X-User-Id` header
   800→   800→   separates data between users but does not verify identity. Suitable for
   801→   801→   local/single-operator deployments; if the API is reachable beyond
   802→   802→   localhost, front it with your own auth proxy. Built-in authentication
   803→   803→   remains future work.
   804→   804→2. **Real-terminal behaviour is implemented but was NOT exercised** — the
   805→   805→   sandbox cannot run MetaTrader 5. All automated tests use a faithful fake;
   806→   806→   use the documented live procedure above to validate a specific broker
   807→   807→   terminal. Until then, treat live behaviour as unverified.
   808→   808→3. **WebSockets/streaming** are not implemented — the frontend polls every
   809→   809→   15–30 s. Planned next.
   810→   810→4. **Three advanced concepts remain intentionally non-operational** —
   811→   811→   `order_block`, `breaker_block` and `support_resistance_reaction` are
   812→   812→   documented in the registry, shown as unavailable in the UI and rejected
   813→   813→   by validation. Their precise definitions, edge cases and tests are
   814→   814→   prerequisite work before activation (the Phase 4 promotions of
   815→   815→   `mss_choch`/`bos`/`liquidity_sweep`/`fvg` show the exact path).
   816→   816→5. **Regime classification is a documented heuristic** — the six regimes
   817→   817→   describe the recent past on one timeframe with configurable thresholds;
   818→   818→   they are not predictions and carry no confidence numbers. Transition and
   819→   819→   uncertain regimes never satisfy a strategy regime requirement.
   820→   820→6. **No probability or confidence percentages** — the report states
   821→   821→   technical conditions and evidence only; a percentage would require a
   822→   822→   defined, validated calibration methodology that does not exist yet.
   823→   823→7. **No automatic trading or order execution** — permanently out of scope;
   824→   824→   nothing here sends orders anywhere, and no profitability is claimed or
   825→   825→   implied.
   826→   826→8. The cache serves at most the configured `limit` of latest candles per
   827→   827→   series; deep historical backfill (beyond the terminal's own depth) is not
   828→   828→   attempted.
   829→   829→9. `docker compose` builds the frontend in dev mode; the production build
   830→   830→   path (`Dockerfile.frontend`) was not exercised inside the sandbox.
   831→   831→
